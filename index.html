The Gateways of Malaysia

After the devastating pandemic of COVID-19 triggered worldwide shutdowns, Malaysia's tourism sector came to a grinding halt.

This visualisation explores when visitors returned to Malaysia, how
they travelled, and what it meant for Malaysia's tourism economy.

Tourism in 2024:

RM291.9b
Tourism industry output
15.1%
Share of Malaysia's economy

These two numbers set the stage: tourism is back.

Source: DOSM, Tourism Satellite Account 2024.

The Comeback

During the lockdown, air travel accounted for most of the limited cross-border movement. However, with the reopening of borders, land travel surged
to capture a 70% share of total traffic.

Since then, a stable pattern has emerged:
land travel maintains its dominant position,
air traffic has settled down at around 30%,
while arrivals via sea rarely surpass 5%.
