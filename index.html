The Gateways of Malaysia

The Comeback

While air travel accounted for the majority of the limited cross-border movement during the lockdown, the reopening of borders triggered
a dramatic reversal. Land travel surged to
capture a 60-70% share of total traffic.

Since then, a stable pattern has emerged:
land travel maintains its dominant position,
air traffic has settled down at around 30%,
while arrivals via sea rarely surpass 5%.
